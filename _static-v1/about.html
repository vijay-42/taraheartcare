Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To deliver honest, evidence-based and affordable heart care, and to make sure no patient with a heart emergency is ever turned away or kept waiting for treatment.

Our Vision

A community where heart disease is detected early, prevented through awareness, and treated with world-class care close to home.

Prevention First

Regular screening and lifestyle guidance to stop disease before it starts.

Ethical Practice

Only the tests and procedures you truly need, explained clearly.

Speed Saves Lives

Rapid response protocols for heart attack and cardiac emergencies.

Compassion

Treating every patient like family, with dignity and respect.

Clinic Infrastructure

Modern Facilities, Designed Around You

Every part of our centre is built for accurate diagnosis, safe procedures and patient comfort.

Advanced Cath Lab

High-resolution flat-panel imaging with low radiation dose for angiography, angioplasty and device implants, available 24/7.

Diagnostic Suite

Digital ECG, colour Doppler 2D Echo, computerised TMT, Holter and ABPM systems, with same-day reports for most tests.

Cardiac Care Unit (CCU)

Fully monitored beds with trained cardiac nursing staff, defibrillators and ventilator support.

Consultation Rooms

Private, comfortable rooms where you can talk openly with your cardiologist.

Patient Comfort

Air-conditioned waiting lounge, clean restrooms, drinking water, wheelchair access and space for family.

Ambulance Support

Coordinated ambulance service with cardiac monitoring for emergency transfers.
