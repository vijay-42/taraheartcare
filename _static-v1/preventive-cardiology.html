Preventive & General Cardiology

Most heart attacks can be prevented. We help you understand your risk and control it before it becomes an emergency.

Hypertension & Cholesterol Management

High blood pressure and high cholesterol are "silent killers": they cause no symptoms until they damage your heart, brain or kidneys. We give you a clear, personalised plan to bring them under control.

  • Accurate BP assessment, including 24-hour ambulatory monitoring (ABPM)
  • Lipid profile evaluation and cardiovascular risk scoring
  • Personalised medication with minimal side effects
  • Diet, exercise and weight-management guidance
  • Diabetes and thyroid co-management

Heart Failure Clinic

Heart failure means the heart is not pumping as well as it should. With the right treatment, most patients feel better, avoid hospital admissions and live longer. Our dedicated clinic provides structured, long-term care.

  • Echo-guided assessment of heart function (ejection fraction)
  • Guideline-directed medical therapy, optimised step by step
  • Fluid, salt and weight monitoring plans
  • Evaluation for CRT / ICD devices where needed
  • Patient and family education
