Non-Invasive Diagnostics

Painless, accurate tests that show how your heart is working, with most reports ready the same day and explained by the cardiologist.

ECG & 2D Echocardiography (Echo)

ECG (Electrocardiogram) records your heart's electrical activity in under 5 minutes. It detects rhythm problems, previous or ongoing heart attacks, and strain on the heart.

2D Echo with Colour Doppler is an ultrasound scan of the heart. It shows the size and pumping strength of your heart (ejection fraction), how the valves work, and whether there is fluid around the heart. It uses no radiation and no needles.

  • Evaluates chest pain, breathlessness, palpitations, murmurs
  • Essential before surgery and for heart-failure follow-up
  • Paediatric and adult echo available
ECG: 5 minEcho: 20–30 minSame-day report

Treadmill Test (TMT) / Stress Test

You walk on a treadmill at increasing speed and incline while we continuously monitor your ECG, heart rate and blood pressure. It shows whether your heart gets enough blood during exercise, which is a key screening test for blocked arteries (coronary artery disease).

  • Screening for coronary artery disease
  • Assessing exercise capacity and fitness
  • Monitored by trained staff with full emergency backup
Approx. 30–45 minWear comfortable shoes

Holter Monitoring & Ambulatory Blood Pressure (ABPM)

Holter monitoring uses a small, portable device worn for 24–48 hours to record every heartbeat during your normal daily routine. It is ideal for catching irregular heartbeats, palpitations or unexplained fainting that a short ECG may miss.

ABPM automatically measures your blood pressure at regular intervals over 24 hours, including during sleep. It confirms true hypertension, rules out "white-coat" high BP, and helps fine-tune medication.

  • Lightweight and comfortable; continue normal activities
  • Detailed analysis report reviewed by the cardiologist
24–48 hoursReturn device next day
